Dungeons Aid — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2, 2026

Dungeons Aid ("the app") is an independent tabletop-RPG companion app developed by Berol99. Contact: dungeonsaid@gmail.com.

The short version: the app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts. Your characters, settings and rules content stay on your device. Data leaves your device only through the three channels described below — one performed by your phone's own speech service, one strictly opt-in, and one you trigger yourself.

1. Microphone and speech recognition

The app's Listen feature transcribes what is said at your table so it can look up game rules for you.

  • The app never records, stores, or uploads audio. No audio ever touches our servers — we don't operate any audio server.
  • By default, transcription is performed by your device's built-in speech recognition service (on Android, typically Google's; on iOS, Apple's). Depending on your device and its settings, that service may process audio on-device or on its provider's servers, under that provider's own privacy policy. This is the same system service used by your keyboard's dictation.
  • Microphone access is used only while you have listening turned on, and only to feed that transcription. You can revoke the microphone permission at any time in your system settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Optional cloud transcription: in Settings you may switch the speech engine to a cloud transcription API (for example OpenAI Whisper) using your own API key and endpoint. If you do, short audio segments are sent directly from your device to the endpoint you configured, under that provider's privacy policy. This mode is off unless you set it up yourself.

The transcribed text is processed on your device to find matching rules entries. It is not sent anywhere, with one opt-in exception described next.

2. Opt-in phrase contributions (off by default)

To improve recognition of D&D terms, you can choose to contribute the text of recognized phrases. This is disabled by default; nothing is collected unless you explicitly turn it on, either at the one-time first-launch prompt or in Settings → Recognition.

If you opt in, the app periodically uploads:

  • the text of phrases recognized while listening (never audio);
  • which compendium entries matched, and whether you opened one;
  • basic technical context: app version and build, operating system and version, device model (a hardware type such as "Pixel 7" — not a serial number), device language, and your recognition settings (engine type, language, sensitivity);
  • a random install identifier — a random number generated inside the app the first time it runs. It is not derived from your device, has no connection to your identity, and resets if you reinstall the app.

This data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase), using Firebase anonymous authentication — no name, no email, no account. We use it for one purpose only: building a corpus to improve the app's D&D-term recognition. It is never sold, shared with third parties (beyond Google's hosting of the database), or used for advertising.

You can withdraw at any time by turning the toggle off in Settings → Recognition. Turning it off also discards anything buffered but not yet uploaded. To request deletion of previously contributed phrases, email dungeonsaid@gmail.com; note that because contributions are anonymous, we may need details (approximate dates, example phrases) to locate yours.

3. Feedback and bug reports (only when you send them)

The in-app feedback form (Settings → Feedback & bug reports) sends your message to the developer through a Google Form. Along with your title, description, and optional name, a diagnostic attachment is included: app version, OS and version, device model, device language, relevant app settings, and the list of loaded compendium files. The app shows you this exact attachment before you send it. Nothing is sent unless you press Send.

If the form is unreachable, the app instead opens your own email client with the same content pre-filled, and nothing is sent until you send the email.

4. Everything else stays on your device

  • Characters, dice rolls, notes, portraits — stored only on your device.
  • Compendium files you import — parsed and cached only on your device.
  • Settings, saved/recent entries, pronunciation fixes, on-device recognition learning — only on your device.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this local data.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone — speech recognition for the Listen feature (see §1).
  • Photos/camera (only when you use it) — to set a character portrait. Portraits never leave your device.
  • Files (only when you use it) — to import compendium XML files or custom fonts you choose.

6. Third-party services

  • Device speech recognition (Google on most Android devices, Apple on iOS) — processes audio for transcription under its provider's policy.
  • Google Firebase (Firestore, anonymous auth) — stores opt-in phrase contributions only (§2).
  • Google Forms — receives feedback reports you send (§3).
  • A cloud transcription provider of your choosing — only if you configure one yourself (§1).

The app contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics SDKs.

7. Children

Dungeons Aid is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The app collects no personal information unless you opt in to text contributions or send feedback, and both are anonymous by design.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters (for example, if the opt-in contribution wording changes), the app will show the consent prompt again before anything new is collected. The latest version of this policy is always available at the URL where you are reading it.

9. Contact

Questions, concerns, or deletion requests: dungeonsaid@gmail.com.
