Less flipping, more playing

Built by a player who got tired of losing the thread of combat to a rulebook search.

Listen

  • Speak naturally. The app picks spells, conditions, monsters and items out of what you say
  • Tap any result to open the full rules
  • Prefer typing? Search is forgiving of typos
  • Speech is processed on your device by default

Character sheets

  • Guided builder: species, class, background, scores, gear
  • AC, saves, attacks and spell slots are computed for you
  • Conditions and exhaustion adjust your rolls automatically
  • Tap to roll; hold and slide for advantage or disadvantage
  • Level-up wizard, companions and summons, session notes

Your content

  • Ships with the free SRD 5.2.1 rules
  • Import any FightClub5-format XML for the books and homebrew you own
  • Build custom monsters, items and spells in the app
  • No account. Your characters stay on your device

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no account.

iPhone?

Not yet. Android comes first; iOS is planned after the public launch. Join the Discord to hear when it's ready.

Which rules are included?

The app ships with the System Reference Document 5.2.1, the free, openly licensed core of the 2024 5e rules. If you own other books or have homebrew in FightClub5 / Game Master 5 XML format, you can import it in Settings → Compendiums.

Does it record my table?

Only while you turn the microphone on. Speech is turned into text on your device by default, and nothing is stored or uploaded unless you opt in to help improve recognition. Read the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Rules lookup, character sheets and dice all work offline. On-device speech recognition depends on your phone's speech engine; most current Android phones support it offline.

“App not available” on Google Play?

Make sure you finished steps 1 and 2 with the same Google account your phone's Play Store uses. It can take a few minutes after opting in before the Play Store lets you install.
